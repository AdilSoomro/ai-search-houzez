Natural Language Meets Real Estate Search

Traditional real estate search requires visitors to manually navigate dozens of dropdowns, checkboxes, and sliders. AI Search for Houzez replaces friction with conversation. Website visitors can simply describe what they need — whether it's a pet-friendly penthouse with a balcony, a suburban family house near top schools, or a waterfront villa under a specific budget. The AI parses the request, extracts precise parameters, and instantly presents matching listings from your Houzez database.

Bring Your Own API Key — Zero Monthly Subscriptions

Unlike third-party SaaS search solutions that charge expensive recurring monthly fees per search or per listing, AI Search for Houzez connects directly to your preferred AI provider using your own API key. You only pay the direct API provider costs (typically pennies per thousands of queries), with complete privacy and full data control:

  • OpenAI: GPT-5-mini, GPT-4o-mini, GPT-4o
  • Anthropic: Claude 3.5 Haiku, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 4.5 Haiku
  • Google: Gemini 3 Flash, Gemini 2.0 Flash, Gemini Flash-Lite

1-Click Elementor Homepage Setup

Getting started takes less than 60 seconds. The built-in 1-Click Homepage Installer creates a ready-to-use "AI Smart Home" page featuring the AI Search Hero widget with customizable suggested prompt pills. With the "Preserve current homepage sections" feature enabled, the installer will automatically keep all your existing Elementor sections (featured listings, agents, testimonials, sliders) intact and cleanly place the AI search hero at the top.

Smart Location Resolver & Alias Engine

Visitors often search using local neighborhood nicknames, abbreviations, or landmarks that might not match the official taxonomy term in WordPress. The built-in Location Resolver bridges this gap by automatically translating aliases (such as "SoHo" → "Manhattan" or "Big Apple" → "New York") into your actual Houzez cities and areas. The plugin also logs any location search misses so you can easily review and create custom aliases over time.

Interactive Filter Tag Chips & Live Refinements

When visitors search, the AI displays interactive tag chips for each detected filter (price range, property type, bedrooms, city, features). Visitors can click any chip to adjust it via a dropdown or remove it entirely to broaden their search — with the property results updating immediately without triggering extra AI API calls.

Built-in Rate Limiting & Cost Safety Caps

Keep your server fast and your API budget secure. Set maximum search limits per user per hour (protecting against bot scrapers) and maximum daily searches for the entire website. When limits are reached, the system gracefully informs visitors while keeping your website operating smoothly.
